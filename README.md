# 📺 YouTube Video Trends Analysis: Categories & Regions

### Elaborado por: Sarah Moscoso🎀

## 🧪Project Description:
This project explores YouTube video trends across different categories and regions using interactive visualizations.
The analysis focused on:

- Identifying the most frequent trending categories
- Analyzing the geographic distribution of trends
- Observing the specific behavior of the United States compared to other regions
- Dashboard: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/julia.ochante/viz/Proyecto12_17522707983480/Dashboard1?publish=yes

## 📝Key Questions & Findings
### 1. Which video categories trended most frequently?
The most common trending categories were:

- Entertainment
- People & Blogs
- Music
- These categories consistently appeared over time, with peaks on specific dates.

### 2. How were trends distributed across regions?
The regions with the highest number of trending videos were:

- United States
- France
- Russia
These countries represented a large share of total trending events.

### 3. Which categories were particularly popular in the United States?
In the U.S., the most prominent categories were:

- Entertainment
- Music
- Howto & Style
Additionally, Comedy showed noticeable increases during certain periods

Differences with other countries:

- In France, Entertainment and People & Blogs were more dominant.
- In Russia, Howto & Style was less relevant compared to the U.S.

### 4. Daily Relative Category Behavior (%)
Interactive charts displayed how each category’s relative share varied over time.
Some categories gained or lost prominence on specific dates, often linked to real-world events.

## 📈 General Conclusions
- Entertainment is the most universal and consistent trending category.
- Clear regional differences exist: some categories dominate in specific countries.
- The U.S. profile is diversified, with strong consumption of entertainment and music.
- Interactive filters allow precise exploration of temporal and regional variations.

## 🛠️Technologies & Tools:
- Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, plotly)
- Interactive dashboards for trend visualization
